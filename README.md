# 🧠 Anki Study Radar

**Study what needs the most attention — at the right time.**

**Anki Study Radar** is an add-on for Anki Desktop that analyzes your review history and helps you discover **which subjects, topics, and decks deserve the most attention today**.

While Anki organizes the review of each card, Study Radar helps you see the bigger picture of your studies.

---

## ✨ What Study Radar does

* Automatically identifies your decks and subdecks.
* Shows which topics are most forgotten or need reinforcement.
* Calculates a study priority from **0 to 100**.
* Analyzes your performance with:

  * Again
  * Hard
  * Good
  * Easy
* Helps you decide **what to review first**.
* Organizes your studies without changing Anki's normal scheduling.

---

## 🎯 Who is it for?

Study Radar is ideal for people who:

* have many decks in Anki;
* study multiple subjects at the same time;
* don't know which subject they should review that day;
