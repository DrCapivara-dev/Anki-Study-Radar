# 🧠 Anki Study Radar

**Study what needs the most attention — at the right time.**

**Anki Study Radar** is an add-on for Anki Desktop that analyzes your review history and helps you discover **which subjects, topics, and decks deserve the most attention today**.

While Anki organizes the review of each card, Study Radar helps you see the bigger picture of your studies.

---

## ✨ What Study Radar does

* Automatically identifies your decks and subdecks.
* Shows which topics are most forgotten or need reinforcement.
* Calculates a study priority from **0 to 100**.
* Analyzes your performance with:

  * Again
  * Hard
  * Good
  * Easy
* Helps you decide **what to review first**.
* Organizes your studies without changing Anki's normal scheduling.

---

## 🎯 Who is it for?

Study Radar is ideal for people who:

* have many decks in Anki;
* study multiple subjects at the same time;
* don't know which subject they should review that day;
* want to identify weak areas more easily;
* want to study in a more targeted way;
* use Anki for exams, college, residency, competitive exams, or long-term studying.

For those who want a deeper view of their studies.

Set an exam date to increase the priority of the most important topics as the exam approaches.

## 🛡️ Your Anki stays your Anki

Study Radar was created to complement Anki.

It **does not replace FSRS** and does not change the normal scheduling logic of your cards.

Anki continues to decide **when each card should appear**.

Study Radar helps you decide:

> **Which subject or topic deserves my attention today?**

---

## 🔒 Privacy

The content of your cards remains on your computer.

Study Radar uses your local review history to generate analyses and recommendations.

Account, license, and activation data are used only for the operation of Study Radar Pro's commercial features.

---

## 💻 Compatibility

Currently available for:

**Anki Desktop**

Study Radar does not work as an add-on on AnkiMobile or AnkiDroid.

---

## 📥 Installation

1. Go to **Releases** in this repository.

2. Download the latest `.ankiaddon` file.

3. Open Anki Desktop.

4. Go to:

   **Tools → Add-ons → Install from file**

5. Select the downloaded file.

6. Restart Anki.

Then open:

**Tools → Study Radar**
