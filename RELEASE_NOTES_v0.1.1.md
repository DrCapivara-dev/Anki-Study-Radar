# Anki Study Radar v0.1.1 — Beta

Anki Study Radar helps you decide **which deck/topic is worth revisiting today**, based on your local Anki review history.

### Highlights
- Automatically reads the user's own deck names.
- Uses meaningful sessions and recent Again/Hard/Good/Easy performance.
- Default thematic spacing: 2 → 4 → 7 → 14 → 21 → 30 → 45 → 60 days.
- Does not change FSRS or card scheduling.
- Cleaner nested deck display: `Parent › Child › Topic`.

This is an early beta. Feedback and bug reports are welcome.
